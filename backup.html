Photos Backup to Folder

Saves your Google Photos into Year / Month folders on a drive you pick.

Before you start

  1. On the memory stick, create an empty folder (for example PhotosBackup). Chrome won't let you pick a drive's top level.
  2. Open photos.google.com in another tab and make sure you're signed in on the main Photos view.
  3. Keep both windows open until the backup finishes. The extension keeps the screen awake, but closing the laptop lid or locking the Mac by hand (Ctrl+Cmd+Q) still pauses it.
