GPhotos Backup

Saves your Google Photos library into a folder on your computer or an external drive, organized the way you like.

Before you start

  1. Create an empty folder where the backup should go (for example PhotosBackup). It can be on your computer or on an external drive. Chrome won't let you pick the top level of a drive, so make a folder inside it.
  2. Open photos.google.com in another window and make sure you're signed in on the main Photos view. Keep it visible while the backup runs.
  3. Keep both windows open until it finishes. The extension keeps the screen awake, but closing the laptop lid or locking the computer by hand will still pause it.
